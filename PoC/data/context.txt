The main office building is open from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. All employees must wear their security badges visibly at all times while on the premises. If you lose your badge, report it immediately to building security on the ground floor. 

Remote work is permitted up to three days a week with direct manager approval. Employees working remotely must use the company VPN for all internal network access. The IT helpdesk is available 24/7 via the online ticketing portal. However, live phone support for IT issues is only available from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. 

Employee parking is located in the East Garage, which is free of charge. Visitors must register at the front desk upon arrival and park in the designated visitor spaces near the main entrance. The company cafeteria is located on the second floor and serves lunch between 11:30 AM and 1:30 PM.
